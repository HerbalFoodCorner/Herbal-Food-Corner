What is Kola Kanda?

Kola Kanda, also written as Kola Kenda, is a traditional Sri Lankan herbal porridge. It is a familiar breakfast food associated with local food culture and the use of leafy greens in everyday cooking.

The Sinhala name කොළකැඳ is commonly used for this herbal porridge. Recipes and ingredients can vary between households and regions.

Traditional herbal porridge in Sri Lanka

Kola Kanda is enjoyed as part of Sri Lanka's traditional breakfast culture. Many families have their own ways of preparing it, using locally familiar ingredients and leafy greens.
